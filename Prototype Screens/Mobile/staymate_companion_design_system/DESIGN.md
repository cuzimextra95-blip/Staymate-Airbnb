## Brand & Style

The design system marries world-class hospitality craft with an intuitive, context-aware AI travel copilot. It builds on an iconic, warm design language—characterized by generous whitespace, human-centric clarity, and authentic hospitality aesthetics—while introducing an iridescent AI dimension that signals synthetic intelligence without sacrificing trustworthiness or warmth.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Modern Minimalist Hospitality**: Soft off-white canvases, immaculate structural alignment, high-legibility typographic hierarchies, and organic rounded forms that evoke physical comfort and architectural calm.
- **Ambient Synthetic Intelligence**: Rather than cold futuristic chrome or stark conversational text boxes, AI interactions introduce luminous, translucent glass layers, soft-focus prismatic glows, and delicate violet-rose chromatic gradients.
- **Tone & Persona**: Warm, discerning, empathetic, and effortless. The interface feels less like software and more like a seasoned local insider curating an unforgettable stay.

### Target Audience
Travelers, families, and remote workers navigating trip planning, booking nuances, neighborhood vetting, and real-time host coordination who demand visual clarity, effortless comparisons, and trustworthy recommendations.

## Colors

The palette balances hospitality heritage with iridescent AI accents and functional data surfaces.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Hospitality Accent (`#FF385C` - Rausch)**: Reserved for primary booking actions, high-priority system actions, active heart states, and brand-defining focal moments.
- **Secondary AI Accent (`#8B5CF6` - Iris Violet)**: Denotes artificial intelligence orchestration, contextual reasoning chips, and ambient assistant statuses.
- **Tertiary AI Accent (`#EC4899` - Electric Rose)**: Combines with Iris Violet to forge the signature gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #8B5CF6 0%, #EC4899 50%, #FF385C 100%)`) that wraps active agent thinking states, copilot badges, and intelligent summaries.
- **Neutral Core (`#222222` - Fog Charcoal)**: Primary text and high-contrast structural glyphs, supported by secondary carbon (`#717171`), subtle borders (`#EBEBEB`), and foundational background surfaces (`#FFFFFF` pure surface, `#F7F7F7` muted background canvas).

### Semantic & Comparison Tones
- **Affirmative & Pros (`#059669` - Emerald)**: Highlights listing perks, competitive price drops, verified superhost attributes, and positive trade-offs.
- **Cautionary & Trade-Offs (`#D97706` - Warm Amber)**: Flags logistical considerations (e.g., steep staircases, lack of dedicated parking, firm cancellation limits).
- **Neutral Comparison (`#64748B` - Slate)**: Used for baseline amenities, standard metrics, and contextual comparisons.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering geometric clarity, subtle modern apertures, and sculpted grotesque curves that mirror the warmth and precision of native hospitality typography.

### Typographic Principles
- **Title Authority**: Display and Headline styles rely on tight negative letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and balanced semi-bold to bold weights to establish immediate structure.
- **Narrative Readability**: Body text uses balanced heights (`line-height: 1.5` to `1.6`) to provide effortless scanning for lengthy property reviews, contextual neighborhood itineraries, and AI assistant dialogues.
- **Functional Labels & Metadata**: Pill tags, badge indicators, price units, and Superhost badges employ medium-to-bold weights with slight tracking expansion to maintain optical legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an 8pt architectural rhythm, with a strict 4pt baseline subdivision for micro-alignments, tag heights, and avatar badges.

### Grid & Compositional Rhythm
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded at a maximum inner width of `1440px`. Column gutters remain `1.5rem` (`24px`), with canvas outer margins at `3rem` (`48px`). The copilot assistant pane docks seamlessly as a 400px fixed-width companion drawer or co-exists within a 4-column span.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. The assistant converts into an expandable bottom drawer or a floating overlay.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins. AI suggestions morph into a persistent, bottom-anchored companion bar.

### Spacing Tokens Application
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-gaps between badge status dots and text labels; nested card inner padding offsets.
- `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-label spacing inside search bars and chip carousels.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard internal card padding, dialog content padding, and input vertical spacing.
- `space-lg` (24px): Structural gap between listing card clusters and tabular comparison column gutters.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section-to-section narrative transitions and full-screen view dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ultra-diffused, ambient shadow profiles layered with subtle translucent hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`). This avoids heavy dropped shadows in favor of natural optical lift.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Pure background (`#F7F7F7` or `#FFFFFF`).
- **Level 1 (Card Resting)**: Standard listing and module cards. Shadow: `0px 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`, framed by a crisp border `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Surfaces)**: Listing card hover, floating pill badges, interactive map markers. Shadow: `0px 6px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Assistant Drawer & Modals)**: StayMate persistent AI prompt container, price breakdown modal, booking checkout summary. Shadow: `0px 16px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12), 0px 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.

### Synthetic AI Depth & Glassmorphism
Elements driven by the copilot employ a frosted glass surface:
- **Surface**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`.
- **AI Aura**: Interactive agent cards and live insight pills feature an ambient, breathing back-glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`.
- **Border**: Border gradients via an ultra-thin mask containing `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)` blending to `rgba(255, 56, 92, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric curves that convey physical tactile ergonomics, approachable warmth, and clean architectural rhythm.

### Geometric Archetypes
- **Standard Corners (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Form inputs, tabular container inner cells, context tooltips, and secondary micro-buttons.
- **Card Corners (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / `16px`)**: Primary property visual cards, review quote panels, and booking breakdown containers.
- **Hero & Assistant Panels (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / `24px`)**: The main StayMate conversation canvas, floating bottom interaction sheets, and top-tier listing galleries.
- **Continuous Curvature (Fully Rounded / Pill / `9999px`)**: Interactive filter chips, search command capsules, Superhost badges, and glowing AI status pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Hospitality Action**: Solid Rausch (`#FF385C`) fill with white text. Height: `48px`, font size: `16px` (`label-lg`), border radius: `8px`. Hover: `#E00B41` with a micro-scale transition of `1.01`.
- **StayMate AI Action**: Gradient fill `linear-gradient(135deg, #8B5CF6 0%, #EC4899 50%, #FF385C 100%)` with white text and a faint violet back-glow. Used for "Generate Itinerary", "Analyze Reviews", and "Find Hidden Trade-Offs".
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pure white surface with a `1px solid #222222` or `1px solid #DDDDDD` outline. Black text. Subtle hover fill at `#F7F7F7`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Filter Chips**: Height `36px`, pill-shaped (`9999px`), `1px solid #DDDDDD`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active state switches to `#222222` fill with white text.
- **AI Context Chips**: Frosted surface with `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)`. Features an Iris Violet sparkle icon prefix with text colored `#6D28D9`. Active state transitions to subtle violet fill (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.1)`).

### AI Agent Status Indicators
- **Thinking / Active State**: An 8px circular status indicator encased in a soft, radiating concentric ring (`animation: pulse 2s infinite`). Uses the Iris Violet to Rausch gradient.
- **Badge Capsule**: Compact pill (`height: 24px`, padding: `0 10px`) containing the micro-dot indicator alongside `label-sm` text: `"StayMate Evaluating"`, `"Local Insight Verified"`.

### Tabular Listing Comparison
- **Matrix Layout**: Clean, border-collapsed grid with `1px solid #EBEBEB`. Alternating column headers feature clean listing thumbnails and titles.
- **Sticky Column Headers**: Fixed upon scroll with background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` and backdrop blur.
- **Row Types**:
  - *Amenity Verification*: Boolean checkmarks (`#059669`) versus missing dashes (`#94A3B8`).
  - *Walking Distance & Transit*: Numerical values highlighted with contextual commute pill tags.
  - *Total Price Clarity*: Prominent bold values including cleaning/service fees.

### Trade-Off Callout Modules (Pros & Considerations)
- **Positive Insight Module (Pros)**:
  - Background: `#F0FDF4` (Emerald tint).
  - Left Accent Border: `3px solid #059669`.
  - Icon: Emerald check badge.
  - Content: Contextual advantages (e.g., "Exceptional soundproofing confirmed in 94% of recent reviews").
- **Trade-Off Module (Cons & Nuances)**:
  - Background: `#FFFBEB` (Amber tint).
  - Left Accent Border: `3px solid #D97706`.
  - Icon: Amber alert beacon.
  - Content: Candid considerations (e.g., "4th-floor walk-up with no elevator access; potential street noise on weekends").

### Cards & Input Fields
- **Listing Cards**: 16px corner radius, edge-to-edge 20:19 aspect ratio photographic carousel, floating top-right wish-list heart with black drop-shadow, bold nightly price typography, and an integrated bottom micro-badge for StayMate rating match.
- **Search Capsules & Inputs**: Floating pill search bar centered at `height: 66px`, divided into three distinct touch segments (Where, When, Who), separated by vertical hair dividers (`#DDDDDD`). Hover introduces subtle `#EBEBEB` surface fills without displacing layout.
